# Rajarata Villa — Luxury Paddy-View Eco Retreat & EV Charging
**Inspired by Rainforest River Song Villa (rainforestriversong.com)**  
**Location:** Anuradhapura, North Central Province, Sri Lanka

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Requirements Delivered

1. **Modern Resort Aesthetic & Architecture (Matching Rainforest River Song)**
   - Fixed floating glassmorphism pill navigation bar with mobile drawer
   - Hero section with high-impact zoom transitions, typography, and subtitle
   - Floating stats bar with 4 metrics: Proximity to Ruwanwelisaya, 22kW EV Solar Fast Charging, 4.95★ rating, and 100% Paddy field panorama
   - Handcrafted suites showcase with interactive image carousel & thumbnail selector
   - Farm-to-table Rajarata dining section with traditional clay pot cuisine
   - Authentic visual moments gallery with lightbox
   - Location section with exact travel times & distance table
   - Interactive FAQ accordion & rich footer

2. **Explore Section (9 Iconic Anuradhapura Attractions)**
   - High-resolution, accurate imagery for all 9 historical destinations
   - Exact distance & travel time from Rajarata Villa on every card
   - Sinhala & English titles
   - Interactive detail modal on click displaying:
     - Exact distance from Rajarata Villa doorstep
     - Drive time & route guidance
     - Key Highlights & Specialties (විශේෂතා)
     - Optimal time of day to visit
     - Cultural etiquette & tips
     - One-click button to request host to arrange tuk-tuk/car/bicycle tour
   - **The 9 Destinations:**
     1. Ruwanwelisaya Maha Seya (4.5 km • 8 mins)
     2. Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi (4.2 km • 7 mins)
     3. Jetavanaramaya Stupa (5.8 km • 11 mins)
     4. Abhayagiri Monastery & Stupa (6.4 km • 13 mins)
     5. Thuparamaya Stupa (4.9 km • 9 mins)
     6. Isurumuniya Vihara & Lovers Rock (3.8 km • 6 mins)
     7. Mihintale Sacred Mountain Peak (14.2 km • 18 mins)
     8. Kuttam Pokuna (The Twin Ponds) (6.8 km • 14 mins)
     9. Mirisawetiya Stupa & Tissa Wewa (3.6 km • 6 mins)

3. **Features Section (EV Fast Charging Station Highlighted)**
   - Dedicated Level-2 Type 2 22kW Fast AC Charging Station
   - 100% clean solar-powered from villa rooftop PV array
   - Compatibility with BYD, Tesla, Hyundai, Nissan Leaf, MG, and Plug-in Hybrids
   - Dedicated shaded bay with 24/7 CCTV surveillance
   - Complimentary overnight charging for staying guests
   - Additional features: High-speed fiber WiFi, solar hot water, paddy veranda, gated parking, driver accommodations.

4. **Complete Frontend Booking Engine (`/booking`)**
   - Live check-in & check-out date picker with dynamic night calculation
   - Guest selector (Adults & Children)
   - Suite selection (Deluxe Paddy-View Suite, Heritage Family Suite, Whole Villa Sanctuary)
   - **EV Charging checkbox toggle** with vehicle model input
   - Add-on experiences (Clay pot breakfast/dinner, guided Atamasthana tour, Wilpattu safari jeep)
   - Currency toggle: USD ($) & LKR (Rs.)
   - Dynamic price breakdown with itemized calculation
   - Guest contact details form
   - Instant confirmation modal with reservation reference (`RV-XXXXXX`) and **Direct WhatsApp booking button** with pre-filled message!

5. **Images Integration**
   - Utilizes authentic photos from `d:\rajaratavilla\images\` for suites, veranda, living hall, dining, bathrooms, and paddy views.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

```bash
# 1. Install dependencies
npm install

# 2. Run the development server
npm run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
